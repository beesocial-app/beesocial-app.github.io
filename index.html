How it works

Tweak is a filter for Safari. Here's what that means, with Instagram as the example.

  1. 1

    Tweak takes out the ads, suggested posts and short videos.

    When a site opens in Safari, Instagram for example, those parts are simply missing. Everything else is exactly as it was.

  2. 2

    You open the site from a new icon on your Home Screen.

    It's the site's own website, Instagram's in this case, and it looks and works just like the app.

  3. 3

    Here's the difference.

    The same feed, with and without Tweak. On the left, a sponsored post slipped in after your friend's, and a Reels tab in the bar. On the right, no ads, no suggested posts and no Reels tab.

    Drawn to the real screens' layout rather than photographed, with no real accounts. On your phone it looks like Instagram, only with less in it.

Setting up takes about a minute, and Tweak walks you through every tap.

The world's best social media feed editor.

  • Focused. It does one job: your feed, the way you want it.
  • Light. It's Safari's own filter, so nothing extra runs on the page.
  • Up to date. The rules are checked every day, and Instagram fixes reach you without waiting for an app update.

Tweak never sees your login, your messages or anything you look at.

You log in on the site itself, in Safari. Tweak gives Safari a list of what to leave out, and Apple designs these filters so they are never told which pages you visit or what's on them. There are no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
