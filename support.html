In the beta

Open the TestFlight app, tap Tweak, then Send Beta Feedback. You can add a screenshot too, even one taken in Safari.

Something not working?

First, close Instagram or YouTube and open it again, because a change only shows on a fresh page. If it's still wrong, report it on GitHub. Please don't include screenshots that show your friends' names or messages.

Something showing that should be hidden?

Check that Tweak's switch is on in Settings, under Apps, Safari, Extensions, and that you opened the new icon on your Home Screen and not the old app. The filter only works in Safari. If you use another content blocker, try it switched off.
