Privacy policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

Bee Social is an iPhone app that hides parts of Instagram's website in Safari. This page explains what it can and can't see. The short version: it collects nothing about you.

What Bee Social can't see

Your browsing

Bee Social is a Safari content blocker. It gives Safari a list of rules, and Safari applies them as pages load. Apple designs content blockers so they are never told which pages you visit or what is on them. In Settings, under Apps, Safari, Extensions, Bee Social, Apple states that Bee Social does not have permission to read or transmit content from any web pages.

Your Instagram account

You log in on Instagram's own website, in Safari. Bee Social never asks for your password and has no way to read it, your messages, your posts or anything else in your account.

What we collect

Nothing. Bee Social has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it sends nothing about you to us or to anyone else.

Your settings, such as what to hide and which page Instagram opens on, are stored only on your iPhone. If you choose your profile as the start page, your Instagram username is stored only on your iPhone too, and is used only to open that page.

The one thing Bee Social downloads

When you open the app, Bee Social downloads the latest list of hiding rules from this website, so fixes reach you when Instagram changes its site. The request contains no information about you. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may record visitors' IP addresses for security. See GitHub's note on data collection.

Testing with TestFlight

If you test Bee Social through Apple's TestFlight app, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with the developer, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. We use these only to fix problems.

A word on other apps

Some apps that offer a calmer Instagram have you log in to Instagram inside the app itself. Depending on how it's built, an app that shows Instagram in its own built-in browser could technically run its own code on the page to read what you type there, and could also read the cookies that keep you logged in. It's worth following Instagram's own advice and only giving your login to apps you trust.

Instagram

Your use of Instagram is covered by Instagram's own terms and privacy policy. Bee Social is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.
